══ 7. ACTIONABLE PROCESS UPGRADES ══
1. Dual-window gate: KEEP only if L7 hit≥6/7 AND season hit≥75% at that exact line.
2. Minutes gate: require L7 avg MIN≥24 (NBA) else demote to RISK / skip.
3. Drift check: if L7 mean − season ≥ +3 on PTS/REB/AST, shave one extra step.
4. Cliff tag: if player has ≥3 games under 22 min this season, never spine without minutes confirmation.
5. Correlation: same-player PTS+AST is OK only if BOTH pass dual-window; don't treat as 1 risk.
6. STL/BLK: research only — never parlay spine.
7. Soft 7/7: still banned — accuracy ≠ juice efficiency.
8. Hot L7 / cold season traps list → auto-shave or CUT from spine.

══ 8. WHAT DID NOT POP (negative findings) ══

· Perfect L7 alone is common and misleading — season window catches most bad spines.
· NHL SOG above 3.5 rarely stays ≥85% season — prefer 2.5 floors.
· Early-season NCAAM not in this sheet universe — keep separate landmine rules.
